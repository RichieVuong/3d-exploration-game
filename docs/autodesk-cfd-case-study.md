# Autodesk CFD Case Study

For a separate thermal-design exercise, I analyzed airflow around a compact electronics enclosure in Autodesk CFD. I prepared the geometry, assigned material properties, defined inlet and outlet conditions, and reviewed temperature and velocity results to identify areas with restricted airflow.

The main design takeaway was that vent placement mattered as much as total vent area. I used the simulation results to compare layouts and recommend a clearer intake path across the warm components.

## Skills demonstrated

- Simulation setup and boundary conditions
- Thermal and airflow result review
- Design iteration based on engineering evidence
- Communicating technical findings in a concise report
